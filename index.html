/ 05   the archive

What we've been up to

Robotics workshop for the students of Bongaigaon Refinery Township, 2026

We conducted a hands-on robotics workshop during July and August for students of BGR HS School and DPS Dhaligaon, Bongaigaon Refinery Township, sponsored by Bongaigaon Refinery. The students learned the basics of MCUs, robotics, and sensors and built 10 robots themselves. We also held a short session on drone building, introducing them to the basics of drone components and working.

Robo Striker hosted by Bineswar Brahma Engineering College, 2026

We participated in the Robo Striker competition at Technisia, hosted by Bineswar Brahma Engineering College, in February, where our team secured 2nd place.

Robo Soccer Competition hosted by NIT Silchar, 2026

We participated in the Robo Soccer competition at Technoesis, hosted by NIT Silchar, in January. Two of our teams competed in the event, securing 2nd and 3rd places.
